Ask us directly
What products does Navyug Exim export?+

We offer onions, fruits, vegetables, spices, frozen items, confectionery, rice, flour, salt, and makhana / fox nuts, among other agricultural commodities.

Can you arrange custom packaging?+

Yes. Share your product, packaging preference, destination country and quantity in your enquiry to discuss available options.

Do you help with export paperwork?+

We coordinate export invoices, packing lists, customs paperwork, and other applicable documents according to the shipment and destination.

How can I get export pricing?+

Use the Request Quote form or WhatsApp us with your required item, quantity, destination and desired packaging. Quotations depend on the product and order requirements.
